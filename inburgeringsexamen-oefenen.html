FAQ

Questions about practicing

Are these mock exams the same format as the real exam?

Yes, question types and structure follow the real inburgeringsexamen and staatsexamen NT2 formats. The content itself is our own, not copied exam material.

What's the difference with DUO's free practice site?

DUO's version is free and official, good for getting familiar with the format. Ours adds an explanation with every answer and downloadable sets you can practice offline.

Can I practice listening and speaking here too?

Yes, all six parts (reading, listening, writing, speaking, KNM and ONA where relevant) have their own practice section above.

Do I need to practice KNM and ONA as well?

KNM applies to almost everyone. ONA only applies if your specific inburgering route requires it, check your own PIP letter from your gemeente if you're not sure.

Is this enough, or do I need coaching too?

Practice builds familiarity with the format. Speaking and writing are the hardest to self-correct, that's usually where a coach makes the biggest difference.
