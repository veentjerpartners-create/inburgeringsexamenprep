Levels

A2 or B1, which one is yours?

Since the 2021 Wet Inburgering, B1 is the default target for most people on the B1-route. A2 is only an option if B1 turns out to be unrealistic, agreed together with your gemeente.

Afschalen / exception route A2

The lower, foundational level

Basic everyday Dutch: simple conversations, short texts, understanding the essentials of KNM (knowledge of Dutch society). Assigned when B1 isn't realistic within your timeline.

Full A2 exam guide →
Default target level B1

The standard most people work towards

More independent, everyday Dutch: following conversations, writing longer texts, handling work and daily life situations. This is what most B1-route trajectories are built for.

Full B1 exam guide →
